## Overview

**Grand Expedition** (opened via Logbooks, found in the Runes of Aldur ocean region) is one of the highest
Div/hour currency farms in PoE2. Unlike standard Expedition encounters found inside regular maps, Grand
Expeditions are dedicated zones built entirely from chain explosives, Remnants, Runic monsters, and reward
chests — with no junk weapon/armor chests since the loot pass.

## Explosive / Remnant Loop

1. You get **15 explosives** per Expedition — plan the chain before detonating.
2. Route explosions so the chain passes through as many **Remnants** as possible.
3. Prioritize Remnants that boost **artifact drops**, **quantity/rarity**, or **chest reward multipliers** —
   these bonuses stack on everything detonated later in the chain.
4. End the chain on **monster markers** and the **highest-value chests** to benefit from all stacked bonuses.
5. Prioritize opening **Currency**, **Unique**, and **Waystone** chests; **Mysterious/Trinket** chests are also
   high value.
6. Killing **Sirus (Fallen Knight of Aldur)** guarantees a Logbook drop — key for sustaining the loop.

## Aldur's Saga Logbooks

- Ideally use Aldur's Saga on a logbook with **4+ rumors that don't contain a boss/event** — bosses/unique
  maps eat your expeditions without giving good rune value.
- Tip: move items in your inventory to preview if your next logbook has 4+ rumors before committing.
- When run correctly you can roughly **self-sustain** your Aldur's Saga supply, or sell logbooks for profit.

### Boss vs. Unique Map Rumors
- **Boss encounters** (avoid unless farming a specific drop): Stardrinker, Origin of the Fall, The Last to
  Fall, End of the Circle.
- **Unique maps**: All That Glitters, Reflective Waters, Almost Paradise, A Good Fellow, Fallen Skies.
- **Fallen Skies** is the one good unique map — it **guarantees an Aldur's Saga**. A "Visions of Paradise" lets
  you run it twice.
- **Stardrinker** spawns Uthred, who drops a quest item for Runeseeker's Call (~900 divine) — only worth doing
  incidentally while traveling for good rumors.

### Rune Tier List (runes do NOT stack — want one of each)
| Tier | Runes |
|------|-------|
| **SS** | Opulent (if you have 8-9 runes ending with Opulent, that's fine even without a "prolif" rune) |
| **S** | Power, Death, Bond, Oath (Bond is the weakest of S tier) |
| **A** | Time, Rebirth (Rebirth is the only blue rune on this list) |
| **B** | Remaining purple runes — not critical, reroll freely if only 3-5 runes |
| **C** | Remaining blue runes |

- Keep rewards of **3+ Divines or higher**; below that is situational (keep 1-2 divine rewards only if the
  map/runes look weak overall).

### Running the Expedition
- Goal: pick up the highest-value purple runes and **proliferate into 7+ runes**.
- Early in the expedition, don't worry about 4-6 rune counts — grab as many good runes as possible.
- Late in the expedition, push for 7+ rune stacks for max loot.
- **Opulent** is the one "GG" rune — loot is substantially worse without it; rush toward it if it appears.
- On any 5/6/7-rune base, **reroll aggressively** since the base itself can never be bad.
- Rule of thumb: always keep 7+ runes near the end of a run; always reroll toward 5 runes at the start if
  there's no good purple rune yet.

## Spending Artifacts — The 4 Vendors

| Vendor | Role | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| **Tujen** | Haggle for raw currency & rare accessories | Best Divine/Exalted flips; practice quick clicks on the timer |
| **Rog** | Craft rare shields/armor | Cheap, good for early gearing |
| **Gwennen** | Gamble for uniques | High risk/reward — chase Mageblood, Headhunter, etc. |
| **Dannig** | Exchange artifacts for logbooks / reroll tokens | Also flip logbooks directly for value |

## Farm Route (Summary)

1. Enter a **Grand Expedition** map (Aldur's Saga logbook if available).
2. Plan the explosive chain to cover the most Remnants before detonating.
3. Detonate along the planned path; clear the monster packs.
4. Loot everything — prioritize artifacts and chest rewards.
5. Convert artifacts via **Tujen**, or trade **Logbooks** with **Dannig** to fund the next run.
6. Repeat, reinvesting profits into better Waystones/tablets over time.

---

## Requirements

- **Minimum build power**: Headhunter-tier or meta build — clear speed and defense matter due to loot density.
- **Budget builds**: stick to 2-mod tablets / lower tiers until geared up.
